# 链接优先反分类(Linking Over Classification)

## 定义

Zettelkasten 方法的核心组织原则不是分类，而是**链接**。Luhmann 明确反对基于主题的系统分类："We decide against the systematic ordering in accordance with topics and sub-topics and choose instead a firm fixed place"（Luhmann, 1981, §II）。固定地址（编号）只是给每个卡片一个"位置"，但这个位置不代表主题归属。

Schmidt 在学术论文中详细分析了这一原则：Luhmann 拒绝系统的分类体系，"instead of subscribing to the idea of a systematic classification system, he opted for organizing entries based on the principle that they must have only some relation to the previous entry without also having to keep some overarching system in mind"（Schmidt, §3）。其结果是"there are no privileged positions in this web of notes: there is no top and no bottom"（Schmidt, §3）。

zettelkasten.de 将其总结为一句话："A Zettelkasten makes connecting and not collecting a priority"（Sascha, Introduction）。

## 因果意涵

分类体系（刚性结构）→ 知识被锁定在预设框架 → 无法跨域连接。
链接体系（柔性结构）→ 知识自由组合 → 新连接产生涌现洞见。

Luhmann 自己将此表述为："One could say: there must be a local solution (i.e. connection or internal fit) only"（Schmidt 引述 Luhmann 卡片 9/8）。
